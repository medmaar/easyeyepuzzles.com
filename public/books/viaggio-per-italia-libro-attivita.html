About this book

Pack your pencil and travel across Italy! This activity book combines crosswords and other puzzles about iconic monuments, little-known historical details and the mysteries of Sicily.

A cheerful, colourful gift for anyone who loves Italy, with puzzles organised by level for steady, enjoyable progress.
