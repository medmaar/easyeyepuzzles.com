About this book

A word search adventure across Italy for seniors and adults. Each chapter opens with a short introduction to its theme, then invites you to hunt for the famous places, dishes and traditions that make Italy unique.

Clear, generous grids make it a comfortable daily companion, perfect for a quiet afternoon or a coffee break.

What's inside

  • Themed chapters with short introductions
  • Clear, easy-to-read grids
  • Puzzles at several difficulty levels
  • Solutions included
