About this book

A crossword book made with care for grandparents and lovers of the French language. Large fonts and high contrast keep the eyes comfortable, and the large pages are easy to handle.

The clues travel from French castles and cheeses to Francophone capitals and festivals, with three difficulty levels (one to three stars).

What's inside

  • Large fonts and high contrast
  • Three levels: one star (easy) to three stars (expert)
  • Large pages that are easy to handle
  • A short fun fact with the puzzles
  • Solutions included
