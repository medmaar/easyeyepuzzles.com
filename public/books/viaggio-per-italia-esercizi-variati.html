About this book

An activity book that turns a trip around Italy into a series of varied puzzles. From crosswords to matching games, from cryptograms to sudoku, each activity guides you through a different region and theme.

Exercises come in three levels (easy, medium and difficult), so beginners and experienced solvers both find the right challenge.
