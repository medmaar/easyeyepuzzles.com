About this book

A complete brain-training workout in Italian. Instead of one puzzle type, this book mixes seven different activities so every session feels fresh and works a different part of the memory.

The puzzles explore Italian fashion, mythology, history and culture, with easy, medium and harder levels so readers can progress at their own pace.
